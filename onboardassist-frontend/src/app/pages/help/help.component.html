Help & Documentation

How to Use

1. Type your question in the chat or use the microphone button to ask via voice.

2. Wait for the AI to process and generate an answer.

3. Follow up with any additional questions you may have.

Sample Questions

  • "What is the dress code?"
  • "How do I setup my email?"
  • "Where can I find the employee handbook?"

Voice Input

Click the microphone icon in the chat or search bar. Speak clearly into your device's microphone. Your speech will be converted to text automatically. Ensure your browser has permission to access the microphone.

Supported Topics

OnboardAssist can help you with IT setup, HR policies, facility information, training schedules, team introductions, and general company FAQs.

For sensitive or highly specific issues not covered here, please reach out to your HR representative.
